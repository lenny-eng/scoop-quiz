# ScoopFit Product-Selector Quiz: SC100 (Scoop®) vs. SC300 (TheraScoop®)

> Source research: scoopfit.com home page, `/products/the-scoop` (SC100), `/products/sc300-therascoop-lateral-trainer` (SC300), and `/pages/faq`. See "Research Notes" at the bottom for the raw facts these questions are built from.

## Purpose
A short quiz to help a ScoopFit visitor decide between the two lateral elliptical trainers:
- **SC100 — Scoop®** — $149, the original, budget-friendly, therapy-focused model
- **SC300 — TheraScoop®** — $279, the professional-grade therapy **and** cardio model

Each question maps its answers to one or both products. Tally the points at the end; whichever product has more points is the recommendation (ties can be broken by weighting the "goal" and "resistance" questions higher, since those are the sharpest product differentiators).

## Research Notes (facts used to build the questions above)

**Shared across both products**
- Zero-impact seated/recumbent lateral elliptical trainers, "identical patented technology found in machines costing $2,000 or more"
- Designed with physical therapists, sports medicine MDs, and orthopedic experts; referenced clinical studies
- 60-day home trial / money-back guarantee (buyer pays return shipping)
- 1-year standard warranty (3-year extended option available)
- Ships within 48 hours (US/UK); Alaska/Hawaii +$79.99
- FSA/HSA eligible on qualifying models
- Minimal one-person assembly with included tools; video tutorials available
- Recommended use: working up to three–four 20-minute sessions/week, alternating direction for balanced muscle engagement

**SC100 — Scoop® ("the original, most budget-friendly")**
- Price: $149 (was $199)
- Weight capacity: 250 lbs
- Unit weight: under 16–18 lbs (site copy varies between the 2026 refresh and legacy spec)
- Dimensions: 16"H x 19"W x 18"D
- Resistance: single adjustable tension knob, none to moderate only — "because Scoop® is designed for therapy, heavy tension is not recommended"
- No carry handle
- Positioning: compact, therapy-focused, ideal for at-home rehab and gentle strengthening
- Rating: 4.8/5 (496 reviews)

**SC300 — TheraScoop® ("the professional standard")**
- Price: $279 (was $299)
- Weight capacity: 315 lbs (FAQ also cites 312 lbs)
- Unit weight: 27 lbs, includes carry handle
- Dimensions: 13.5"H x 23.25"W x 24"D — lower profile, more likely to fit under a desk
- Resistance: 10 true magnetic settings, light therapy through high-intensity cardio
- Wider, strapped pedals for stability
- Positioning: professional-grade therapy **and** cardio conditioning; found in clinics/professional facilities, also popular for home use
- Rating: 4.7/5 (1,800+ reviews)

**Key differentiators to weight most heavily in scoring**
1. Resistance range / intended intensity (therapy-only vs. therapy + cardio)
2. Weight capacity (250 vs. 315 lbs)
3. Price ($149 vs. $279)
4. Portability/profile (lighter, no handle vs. slightly heavier with carry handle and lower under-desk profile)
