## Questions

### 1. Setting — who are you buying for?
- I'm buying for a gym or other heavy-use commercial setting. **(HLT3500 or HR3500)**
- I'm a physical therapist or clinician buying for my practice. **(HR3500 or SC300)**
- I'm buying for a small, private gym or light-commercial setting such as a hotel. **(HLT3500)**
- I want the same technology clinics use, but at a home-friendly price. **(SC300)**
- This is strictly for personal, at-home use. **(SC100, SC300, H905, or H1000)**

### 2. Body position
- I prefer to be seated while I exercise. **(SC100, SC300, or HR3500)**
- I want to stand and train for a more intense, whole-body workout. **(H905, H1000, or HLT3500)**

### 3. Recovery, balance & joint health
- I'm recovering from an injury or surgery, or I'm deconditioned and need to start gently. **(SC100 or HR3500)**
- I have balance issues and I'm concerned about falling. **(SC100, SC300, or HR3500)**
- I need zero-impact movement that won't aggravate bone-on-bone joints. **(SC100, SC300, or HR3500)**

### 4. Resistance & intensity
- I don't mind controlling resistance with a physical knob instead of a computer. **(SC100 or H905)**
- I want digitally-controlled resistance levels I can progress through over time. **(SC300, H1000, HLT3500, or HR3500)**
- I want the most intense full-size, standing workout possible. **(H905, H1000, or HLT3500)**
- I want the most resistance available in a portable, seated machine. **(SC300)**
- I want the maximum resistance and the most advanced electronic control, period. **(HLT3500 or HR3500)**

### 5. Weight capacity
- I, or another potential user, weigh over 300 lbs. **(HLT3500 or HR3500)**
- I, or another potential user, weigh 250–300 lbs. **(SC300, H905, or H1000)**
- I weigh under 250 lbs and just want the simplest option. **(SC100)**

### 6. Power & tech
- I don't want to plug my machine into an electrical outlet. **(SC100, SC300, or H905)**
- I want a built-in touchscreen or console with real-time stats. **(H1000, HLT3500, or HR3500)**
- I want to take classes on my phone or tablet. **(H905 or H1000)**
- I want to take classes directly on the machine, no phone or tablet needed. **(HLT3500)**
- I'm fine with a simple display and no smart features. **(H905)**

### 7. Portability & space
- I want the lightest, most portable option to store away or travel with. **(SC100)**
- I want portability with more resistance range for a growing routine. **(SC300)**
- I want a permanent, full-size home gym machine. **(H905, H1000, HLT3500, or HR3500)**
- I plan to pedal regularly at my desk while I work. **(SC300)**

### 8. Budget
- I'm on a budget and want the least expensive way to try lateral training. **(SC100 — $149)**
- I want a mid-range portable option with more features. **(SC300 — $279)**
- I'm on a budget but want a full-size machine, no bells and whistles. **(H905 — $2,499)**
- I'm ready to invest in a premium, interactive home machine. **(H1000 — $3,999)**
- I want the top-of-the-line commercial-grade machine, regardless of price. **(HLT3500 or HR3500 — $4,999)**

### 9. Warranty
- I prefer the longest warranty available. **(HLT3500 or HR3500)**
- I want to add extended protection to a portable Scoop. **(SC100 + 3-yr Scoop Protection Plan, or SC300 + 3-yr TheraScoop Protection Plan)**

### 10. Color
- Color is important to me and I want a silver machine. **(H1000)**
- Color is important to me and I want a black machine. **(HR3500 or HLT3500)**
- Color is important to me and I want a white machine. **(H905)**
- *(No color variants found for SC100/SC300 on scoopfit.com.)*

## Changes from the previous draft (HLT300/HLT3000 → HLT3500, HR1000 → HR3500)

- **Contradiction resolved:** "silver machine" previously mapped to `HR1000 or H1000`. HR3500 is documented as black (both the original Helix list and the live HLT3500/HR3500 product family point to a black finish), so it was dropped from the silver line rather than create a machine recommended as both silver and black. Confirm this is right.
- **Removed as redundant:** "best value seated recumbent for home use (HR1000 — $1,799)" — with HR1000 gone, HR3500 is the only recumbent, and it's already covered by the "top-of-the-line commercial-grade" budget line at $4,999. There is no longer a budget/mid-tier seated option other than the portable SC100/SC300.
- **Deduped, no content lost:** several lines that listed both HR1000 and HR3500 (seated preference, recovery, balance, zero-impact, resistance progression, touchscreen, weight 250–300 lbs) now list HR3500 once instead of twice.
- **Net effect:** lineup goes from 7 products to 6 (SC100, SC300, H905, H1000, HLT3500, HR3500).
